# Coffee Orders Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes 1,000 coffee order line items (957 unique orders) from January 2019 to August 2022. It combines lookup formulas, PivotTables, charts, and slicers to show sales trends over time, performance by country, and the top customers.

Business Questions

How have total sales changed over time, and which coffee types drive them?

Which countries generate the most revenue?

Who are the top 5 customers by sales?

How do roast type, size, and loyalty card status affect sales?
